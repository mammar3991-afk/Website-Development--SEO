Real-time fraud verification calls for a leading bank in Pakistan

The situation

As digital payment volumes grew, the bank needed to verify suspicious card, e-commerce, interbank, instant-payment and bill-payment transactions without adding manual work for call-centre agents.

What we built

An outbound robo-call system built on the Octavebytes campaign-management platform and connected to the bank's fraud risk system. When a payment is flagged, the customer receives an automated, personalised call in Urdu or English with the transaction details. They press 1 to confirm or 2 to report fraud. If the call is not answered, the system retries. The result goes back to the fraud system in real time so unauthorised transactions can be blocked.

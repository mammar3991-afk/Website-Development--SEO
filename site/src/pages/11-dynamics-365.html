Amazon Connect for Dynamics 365

The Amazon Connect softphone inside Microsoft Dynamics 365, built on Channel Integration Framework (CIF) 2.0 with Omnichannel support. Callers are matched to leads, accounts and contacts, and every call is logged as a phone-call activity.

What agents can do

  • Softphone in DynamicsAnswer, hold, resume, transfer, mute and conference from the CIF panel.
  • Screen-popThe caller's number is searched across the phone fields you configure on leads, accounts and contacts. A single match opens the record; multiple matches let the agent choose.
  • Click-to-callFrom any customer phone number, or type a number into the panel.
  • Agent statesReady, Not ready and Break, in sync with Amazon Connect.
  • See who is free before transferringAn available-agent list appears before a call transfer.
  • Pause and resume recordingSo sensitive details are not captured.
  • Wrap-up codes and outbound queue selectionCodes after every call, and a queue choice before dialling.
  • Automatic phone-call activityLinked to the matched caller.

Outbound campaigns. Outbound dialling includes queue selection, so agents choose the queue before they call.

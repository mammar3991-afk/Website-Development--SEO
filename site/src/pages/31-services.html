Amazon Connect

Amazon Connect setup and custom builds

Amazon Connect (now called Amazon Connect Customer by AWS) lets you run a cloud contact center you pay for by usage. We set it up, migrate your existing contact center onto it, and build the parts AWS does not give you out of the box.

What we do

Set up and migrate

Design and launch a new Amazon Connect contact center, or migrate from an on-premise or legacy cloud system in phases so service is not interrupted. Includes flows, queues and routing, telephony and numbers, and testing.

Build what is missing

  • Queue selection for outbound calls (agents choose which queue's caller ID to use)
  • Dynamic caller ID by campaign or region
  • Reporting with Amazon QuickSight
  • Real-time wallboards with Octave Vision
  • Lambda functions and IVR logic
  • Messaging channels (WhatsApp Business API, SMS)
  • Agent desktop customisations

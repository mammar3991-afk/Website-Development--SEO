Partners

Partner with Octavebytes

We are a specialist Amazon Connect team: services, migrations, bots and AI, and products built on Amazon Connect. Partners work with us in three ways.

Three ways to work with us

1. Refer

Send us a customer who needs an Amazon Connect migration, build, bot, CRM connector or regulated-sector voice project. Referral terms are agreed per opportunity.

2. Resell

Add our connectors and Campaign Manager to your contact center offering. You keep the customer relationship; we handle deployment and support. Reseller terms are agreed per opportunity.

3. Add delivery capacity

Amazon Connect and AWS engineers who join your project team, remotely, by the hour or by the month. We work under NDA and follow your process, security requirements and tools. Rates are agreed per engagement.

What partners get

  • A named contact at Octavebytes
  • Sales and technical enablement
  • Access to the trial environment for your customers
  • Co-selling through AWS ACE

What we ask of partners

  • A real customer or opportunity
  • Willingness to sign an NDA and a short agreement
  • Honest handling of AWS and HubSpot terms

Legal

Cookie policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how octavebytes.com uses cookies and similar technologies, and how to control them. See also our privacy policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your choices and measure how it is used.

2. Cookies we use

CategoryExamplesPurposeWhen set
EssentialYour cookie choice (stored in your browser)Remembers whether you allowed analytics, so we do not ask on every page.Always
AnalyticsGoogle Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*)Counts visits and shows which pages are useful. IP addresses are anonymised.Only after you choose "Allow analytics"
AnalyticsMicrosoft Clarity (_clck, _clsk)Records anonymised session replays and heatmaps so we can see where visitors get stuck. Form fields are masked.Only after you choose "Allow analytics"
Marketing and CRMHubSpot tracking (__hstc, __hssc, __hssrc, hubspotutk)Links your visits to your contact record if you submit a form, so our team can follow up properly.Only after you choose "Allow analytics"

Forms and the booking page are provided by HubSpot, which may set its own cookies on its pages. See HubSpot's cookie information for details.

3. Your choices

  • When you first visit, choose "Essential only" or "Allow analytics". Essential only means no analytics or marketing cookies are loaded.
  • To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser settings; the banner will appear again.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work if you block essential storage.

4. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. See the rights section of the privacy policy.

5. Changes

We may update this policy when our tools change. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

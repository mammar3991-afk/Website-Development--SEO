Questions we hear

Do we need Amazon Connect already?

No. We can design and launch a new Amazon Connect contact center, or migrate you from your current system. If you already run it, we extend it. See what we do.

How do you migrate without disrupting service?

In phases: build in parallel, move numbers in batches, retire the old system last. See how a migration runs.

Who pays for what?

For projects, you pay Octavebytes a fixed estimate or hourly rate. For products, a per-agent subscription. You pay AWS for Amazon Connect usage and phone numbers. See the cost breakdown.

Where does the software run, and where does my data go?

In your AWS account, in the region you choose. Read about security.

Paid plans include 24/7 support. Projects can be followed by a support agreement.

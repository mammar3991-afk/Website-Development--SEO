What we build

Fraud-verification calls

When your fraud system flags a payment, the customer gets an automated call with the transaction details. Press 1 to confirm, press 2 to report fraud. The answer goes back to your fraud system in real time so suspicious payments can be blocked. Built for card, e-commerce, interbank transfers, instant payments and bill payments.

Payment reminders and collections

Predictive, preview and agent-assisted campaigns with retry logic and time-zone-aware calling.

AI voice agents

Inbound and outbound assistants that answer questions and hand off to a human agent. We build on Amazon Bedrock and integrate with other voice-AI platforms where your team already uses them.

How it works

  1. Use cases first

    We agree the flows and languages in a written requirements document with you.

  2. Integration

    We connect to your fraud, CRM or core-banking systems through your APIs, for example a balance-inquiry API or a hand-off to your contact-centre platform.

  3. Deployment where you need it

    In your cloud account, or on your own servers or virtual machines on request.

  4. Test, then go live

    Testing with your team, then go-live with our engineers on the thread. Schedule, retry and review results in one place.

What we need from you

  • A short requirements document listing use cases and call scripts
  • API endpoints and documentation for the systems we connect to
  • Network and server access agreed with your security team
  • A named technical owner on your side

Languages

Urdu and English are delivered today. More languages (for example Arabic) are available on request.

Security and compliance

Campaigns run inside day-and-time calling windows that you set, with retry rules per call outcome. Ask us about audit, encryption and data residency for your environment.

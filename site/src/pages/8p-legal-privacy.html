Legal

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how Octavebytes ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit octavebytes.com, contact us, request a trial or demo, or book a call. It applies to this website. Our software products are provided under separate agreements, and customer data processed inside a customer's own AWS account is governed by those agreements.

1. Who we are

Octavebytes is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, work email, phone number, company, country, product of interest and the message you write, when you complete a form or book a meeting.
  • Information collected automatically: pages viewed, referring page, campaign (UTM) tags, device and browser type and approximate location from your IP address. Analytics and marketing cookies are used only if you allow them (see our cookie policy).
  • Correspondence: emails and call notes when you talk to our sales or support team.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, set up a trial or demo and provide quotes and support.
  • To manage our customer and prospect records and sales follow-up.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To send product or service communications, only where you have agreed or where the law allows. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and to meet legal obligations.

4. Legal bases (UK and EU visitors)

We rely on your consent (analytics and marketing cookies, marketing emails), on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (trial, quote), and on our legitimate interests in running and improving our business and responding to enquiries.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it with service providers who help us run the website and our business, under written terms: HubSpot (CRM, forms, meetings and email), Google (analytics and email and calendar services), Microsoft (Clarity, anonymised session replays) and Amazon Web Services (hosting and infrastructure). We may also share data where required by law or to protect our rights, or with a successor in a merger or sale of the business.

6. International transfers

We operate in the United States, the United Kingdom and Pakistan, and our providers may process data in other countries. Where data leaves the UK or EU, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer records for as long as needed for the purpose they were collected, and then for a limited period for legal, tax and dispute purposes. Prospect records that have been inactive for an extended period are reviewed and deleted or anonymised. Analytics data is kept in line with our analytics provider's retention settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). California residents have the right to know, delete and correct their data and to opt out of sale or sharing; we do not sell or share personal data for cross-context advertising. To use any right, email us. We may need to verify your identity first.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls and encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Children

This website is for business users and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

What the work is

Everything we do sits on Amazon Connect, the AWS contact center service. Day to day, that means four kinds of work:

  • Contact center builds and migrations. Call flows, routing, telephony and reporting for customers moving to Amazon Connect, and phased migrations from older platforms.
  • CRM connectors. Our own products that put the Amazon Connect softphone inside HubSpot, Dynamics 365 and other CRMs.
  • Bots and voice AI. Chatbots, voice bots and agent assist built on AWS AI services. See bots and AI.
  • Outbound and regulated voice. Campaign tooling and voice systems for banks and regulated businesses.

Who tends to fit

People who like working close to the customer's problem. Useful backgrounds include AWS development, contact center or telephony engineering, CRM development on HubSpot or Dynamics 365, and customer support for technical products. You do not need all of them.

What we do

We help organisations move to Amazon Connect, build on it and add bots and AI. We also build products for it: connectors that put the Amazon Connect softphone inside HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics 365, and a campaign manager. Our engineers deploy everything into your own AWS account and stay with you until it works.

HubSpot CTI screen-pop, explained

Screen-pop sounds simple until you ask what happens when a phone number matches three contacts, or none. Here is exactly how the connector handles each case.

One match

The connector looks up the caller's number, finds a single matching HubSpot record, and opens it automatically — the contact, or its associated company or deal, depending on how your admin has configured screen-pop.

Multiple matches

If more than one HubSpot record shares that phone number, the agent sees a short picker inside the connector window with each match's name and email. They choose the right one, and the call activity binds to that record for the rest of the interaction.

No match

What your admin controls

  • Whether screen-pop searches contacts, companies or deals
  • The placeholder name used for new, unmatched contacts, if applicable
  • Which HubSpot object types are eligible for the search in the first place

More detail lives in the deployment and admin configuration guide.

In one paragraph

Octavebytes is an AWS Select Tier Services Partner, an Amazon Connect Service Delivery Partner and an Amazon Connect Ready Partner. We deliver Amazon Connect migrations, custom builds, bots and voice-AI projects, and we build products on it: connectors for HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics 365 (AWS FTR-certified, listed on AWS Marketplace) and a campaign manager.

What the customer pays

Our plans are per-seat tailored to the customer's team, with a {{var:trial_days}}-day free trial. Call intelligence (transcripts, sentiment, AI summaries) is an add-on. We send a quote or estimate on request. The customer separately pays AWS for Amazon Connect and hosting. Most customers transact through an AWS Marketplace private offer. How that works →

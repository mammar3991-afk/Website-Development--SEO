AWS Marketplace private offers, explained

Buying the connector through AWS Marketplace usually means a private offer rather than a public checkout. Here is how that works.

What a private offer actually is

It is a custom AWS Marketplace listing scoped to one buyer, at agreed pricing and terms. Once you accept it, the charge appears on your normal AWS bill and draws down against any existing AWS spend commitment you have. No separate vendor relationship to set up, no separate invoice to route through a different approval process.

Purchase orders

We generate a private offer against a purchase order and the AWS account ID that will pay. For multi-phase engagements, that can be split into two offers — one covering the first billing period plus a deposit on any professional services, and a second for the remainder once the work is signed off.

One product, one active agreement

AWS Marketplace allows exactly one active subscription per product per buyer account. If you are renewing or changing seat count and see an error saying an offer cannot be accepted because a subscription already exists, that is why — contact us and we will either time the new offer to your current term's end or revise the existing agreement instead of issuing a new one.

How to start

  1. Send your AWS account ID and PO to {{var:sales_email}}
  2. We generate a private offer scoped to that account
  3. Accept it from your own AWS Marketplace console — no separate vendor onboarding
  4. Charges appear on your existing AWS bill and count toward committed spend

Campaign Manager for your contact center

One place to run outbound campaigns across voice, SMS and email: choose the audience, set the schedule and retry rules, launch, and track the results. It can be integrated with Amazon Connect, Genesys, Avaya and other contact center platforms, so campaigns run on the system you already use.

Request a demo

What it does

  • Voice, SMS and emailRun campaigns on each channel from the same admin.
  • Four voice modesAutomated calls with IVR menus, preview dialling, progressive and predictive dialling for agents.
  • SchedulesCalling windows by day and time, a start and end date, or no end date. Priority between campaigns.
  • Retry rulesRetry by outcome, with a delay and a maximum number of attempts.
  • Answering machine detectionTell a person from a voicemail and handle each.
  • AudienceUpload contact lists from CSV, tag contacts with labels, and build segments from filter rules. Configurable customer fields.
  • TemplatesReusable SMS and email templates.
  • Call resultsResults are sent to your own systems by webhook, for example a CRM or a fraud system.
  • Team adminTeams, agents, queues, roles, phone numbers (DIDs), and business-unit separation.

Who it is for

Banks and financial institutions, contact centers and BPOs that need scheduled, auditable outreach: reminders, collections, verification and OTP calls, alerts and surveys. See our delivered work: banking and regulated voice.

Works with your contact center

Campaign Manager is not tied to one platform. It can be integrated with Amazon Connect, Genesys, Avaya and other contact center systems. Campaigns run with your own agents, queues and phone numbers, and results flow back to Campaign Manager. We scope each integration with you. See our Amazon Connect services

## The gap we close

**Amazon Connect handles the call. Your team lives in the CRM.**

Without a connector, agents switch tabs to find who is calling, type notes by hand, and managers cannot see conversations on the customer record.

With Octavebytes, the softphone sits inside your CRM. A call arrives, the right record opens, and the call is logged when it ends.

---

## What your agents get

- **Softphone in the CRM.** Answer, hold, transfer, mute and conference without leaving HubSpot or Dynamics 365.
- **Screen-pop.** The caller's contact, company, deal or ticket opens on arrival. Two matches? The agent picks one. No match? They create a record in a click.
- **Click-to-dial.** Call any number in the CRM.
- **Automatic logging.** Every call becomes an activity with direction, duration and recording.
- **Pause and resume recording** during payments or sensitive details.
- **Wrap-up codes** so outcomes are reported the same way every time.
- **SMS and WhatsApp** from the contact record (Plus plan).
- **Transcripts and AI summaries** written to the record (Plus plan).

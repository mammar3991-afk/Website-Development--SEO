# Call transcripts and AI summaries, on the customer record

Managers cannot listen to every call. Call intelligence writes the parts that matter onto the CRM record, so anyone can see what was said.

## What gets captured
- **The recording**, playable from the call activity
- **A transcript** of the conversation
- **Sentiment** for the call, using Amazon Connect Contact Lens
- **A structured AI summary** (the key points, in a consistent format) generated after the call

## Where it goes
Onto the **call activity in HubSpot** or the **phone-call activity in Dynamics 365**. In HubSpot, transcripts can be placed in the fields HubSpot's own AI features read. `[CONFIRM: what exactly is live for Breeze AI fields]`

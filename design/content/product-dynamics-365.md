# Amazon Connect for Dynamics 365

The Amazon Connect softphone inside Microsoft Dynamics 365, built on Channel Integration Framework (CIF) 2.0 with Omnichannel support. Callers are matched to leads, accounts and contacts, and every call is logged as a phone-call activity.

## What agents can do

- **Softphone in Dynamics.** Answer, hold, resume, transfer, mute and conference from the CIF panel.
- **Screen-pop.** The caller's number is searched across the phone fields you configure on **leads, accounts and contacts**. Single match opens the record; multiple matches let the agent choose.
- **Click-to-call** from any customer phone number, or type a number into the panel.
- **Agent states.** Ready, Not ready, Break, synced with Amazon Connect.
- **See who is free before transferring.** Available-agent list before a call transfer.
- **Pause and resume recording** so sensitive details are not captured.
- **Wrap-up codes** after every call.
- **Outbound queue selection** before dialling.
- **Automatic phone-call activity** linked to the matched caller.

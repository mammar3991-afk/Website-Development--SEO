Built for card, e-commerce, interbank transfers, instant payments and bill payments.

# Amazon Connect setup, migration and custom builds

Amazon Connect (now called Amazon Connect Customer by AWS) lets you run a cloud contact centre you pay for by usage. We set it up, move your existing contact centre onto it, and build the parts AWS does not give you out of the box.

**[Talk to an engineer]**

*Octavebytes is an AWS Select Tier Services Partner, an Amazon Connect Service Delivery Partner and an Amazon Connect Ready Partner.*

## What we do

### Set up and migrate
Design and launch a new Amazon Connect contact centre, or migrate from an on-premise or legacy cloud system in phases so service is not interrupted. Includes flows, queues and routing, telephony and numbers, and testing.

### Connect it to your CRM
Our connectors for [HubSpot](/products/hubspot/) and [Dynamics 365](/products/dynamics-365/) are the fastest route. For any other CRM, we build a custom integration. [Other CRMs →]

### Build what is missing
Typical custom builds:
- **Queue selection** for outbound calls (agents choose which queue's caller ID to use)
- **Dynamic caller ID** by campaign or region
- **Reporting** with Amazon QuickSight
- **Real-time wallboards** with Octave Vision, our wallboard for Amazon Connect
- **Lambda functions and IVR logic**
- **Messaging channels** (WhatsApp Business API, SMS)
- **Agent desktop** customisations `[CONFIRM: Octave Agent Desktop is still offered]`

### Get telephony right
Numbers, carriers and SIP trunks are the most common blockers in Amazon Connect projects. We help you choose and connect them, including international dialling. [Guide: getting phone numbers and carriers right →]

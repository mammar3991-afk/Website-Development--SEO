## Markers used in the copy

| Marker | Meaning | Count |
|---|---|---|
| `[CONFIRM: …]` | A fact or promise the owner must verify before it is published | 73 |
| `[PROOF NEEDED: …]` | Needs customer permission or evidence | 12 |
| `[OWNER DECISION]` | A commercial choice only the owner can make | 3 |

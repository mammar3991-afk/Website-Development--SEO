## 4. How to ask

**Who:** the account manager who handled the customer, with the owner's sign-off on wording.
**When:** 30 days after go-live, after a successful renewal, or right after an issue is fixed well. **Never** in the middle of an open dispute.
**Ask in three tiers** so a "no" to the biggest ask still yields something:
1. **Tier A:** "May we describe your industry, size and use case without your name?"
2. **Tier B:** "May we use your name and logo?"
3. **Tier C:** "May we quote you and publish the results?" `[+ 3-question survey below]`

**Survey (3 questions, plain):**
1. What problem were you trying to solve when you found us?
2. What changed once it was live? (Give a number if you can.)
3. What would you tell a team considering it?

**Store the permission** on the HubSpot company record: *what is approved, who approved it, date, review date*. Review every 12 months. Take the item down on request within 2 business days.

**Incentive:** none by default. If the owner wants to offer one (a month's credit, a co-marketing feature), decide it before asking, and disclose it in the case study if it applies. `[OWNER DECISION]`
